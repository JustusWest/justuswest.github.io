# Research experience

- Fall 2021 - Spring 2026: Graduate Research Assistant, Temple University, Department of Computer and Information Science
  - Advisor: Dr. Eduard Dragut
  - Developed PatchScout, a multi-agent LLM framework that frames thematic web data collection as budget-constrained information foraging; outperforms commercial deep-research systems on open-ended entity collection tasks ([NeurIPS 2026](/publication/NeurIPS2026)).
  - Built an interactive, human-in-the-loop system for thematic data collection on the live web ([VLDB 2026 demo](/publication/VLDB2026Demo)).
  - Developed a data science system to identify postsecondary autism support programs from their websites ([JADD 2025](/publication/JADD2025)).
  - Conducted 34 stakeholder interviews to examine how autistic students discover and use support programs ([Frontiers in Education 2026](/publication/Frontiers2026)).
  - Contributed to an interdisciplinary NSF-funded project applying AI to improve vocational outcomes for autistic adults.

- 2018 - 2020: Undergraduate Research Assistant, Loyola Marymount University, Departments of Psychology and Computer Science
  - Advisors: Dr. Joseph LaBrie, Dr. Andrew Forney
  - Led an undergraduate development team to build CampusGANDR, a cross-platform mobile app delivering personalized normative feedback to first-year college students ([Addictive Behaviors 2025](/publication/Labrie2025)).
  - Oversaw deployment to three cohorts of participants across 21 campuses.
  - Utilized the Google Cloud Platform to develop cloud based machine vision tools for image analysis.
  - Personal research project utilizing Reinforcement Learning agents in the Iterated Prisoner's Dilemma, supervised by Dr. Andrew Forney.
