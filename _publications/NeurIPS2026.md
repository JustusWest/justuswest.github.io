Researchers often need web corpora that are not answers to a single query, but reusable collections of documents spanning many decentralized sources. PatchScout formulates this thematic web data collection task as a long-horizon information foraging problem over query-induced semantic patches, and instantiates a class of patch-switching policies that decide when to keep exploiting a region of the web and when to move to a new one. Across four live-web topics, PatchScout substantially improves yield and domain coverage over traversal-based and search-augmented reasoning agent baselines, and in an applied social science setting it discovers 102 previously unidentified entities.
